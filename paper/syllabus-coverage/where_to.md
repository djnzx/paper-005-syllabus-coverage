# Where to submit — candidate journals (international, Q2–Q3, feasible)

**Paper profile.** A methodological + small-empirical study: a cross-lingual,
explicit-gap method that audits a cybersecurity training curriculum against NIS2 and
ISO/IEC 27001 clauses using sentence embeddings (LaBSE), evaluated on a hand-labelled
real dataset (≈47 clauses, real curricula), with baselines, CIs, and a cross-lingual
test. Contribution is applied/engineering, not a new model. Scope is niche
(compliance × NLP × security training).

**Honest framing of "Q3/Q4".** In this exact niche there are very few *reputable*
Scopus journals sitting purely at Q3/Q4 — most good-fit applied venues are **Q2 by their
best category** (often Q2–Q3 in the relevant *Computer Science Applications* category).
Those Q2 venues are still highly **feasible** for a well-written pilot with honest
limitations, so they are the realistic target. One genuine Q3 option is listed too.
Avoid the temptation of low-bar "Q4/unranked" venues — they read as weak and risk
predatory flags.

**Not targeting Ukrainian journals** (per your instruction; the Ukrainian translation is
not submission-grade). All options below are English-language international venues — drop
the Ukrainian *Анотація* for these, or keep it only if the journal explicitly permits a
second-language abstract.

## Recommendation

- **First choice: Information & Computer Security (Emerald)** — the scope matches the
  paper almost line-for-line (compliance, standards, security education), it's Q2 and
  feasible, and the green route avoids an APC.
- **If you want open-access speed: JCP (MDPI)** — low APC, fast, good fit.
- **Genuine Q3 with broad scope: Security and Privacy (Wiley)** — if the high APC is
  acceptable.
- Keep **SN Computer Science** as a safe fallback (broad, accepting, reputable).

## Caveats and before-you-submit notes

1. **Finish the empirical study first.** Every option above expects real measured results
   (gap-recall/precision, CIs, baseline comparison). Do **not** submit on the toy worked
   example — that is the difference between Q2 acceptance and desk-reject.
2. **"Best quartile" overstates.** A journal listed Q2 in its best category may be Q3 in
   *Computer Science Applications* (our category). Verify the *relevant-category* quartile
   on scimagojr.com before deciding.
3. **MDPI perception.** MDPI venues are Scopus/WoS-indexed and Q-rated, but some
   committees view them skeptically; weigh this against their speed and OA reach. (You
   already cite Electronics [19], so it's consistent with your reference base.)
4. **English-only.** Drop the Ukrainian abstract for these venues unless a second-language
   abstract is explicitly allowed; ensure a native/professional English edit of the final
   manuscript.
5. **Format conversion.** Convert to the venue template (most accept LaTeX or Word). The
   figures already export as PDF (LaTeX) and PNG (Word) from `experiment/`.
6. **All metrics/quartiles here are "latest available (2024)"** from SCImago mirrors —
   re-confirm on scimagojr.com at submission time, as they shift yearly.
